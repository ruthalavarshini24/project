1. Problem Statement & Research Motivation

In modern E-Commerce platforms (such as Flipkart, Amazon, and Myntra), Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) has increased by over 60% in recent years. Retaining an existing customer is approximately 5x to 7x cheaper than acquiring a new one.

Core Objective: Develop an end-to-end Early Warning Classification System that evaluates behavioral telemetry (account age, order frequency, monthly spend velocity, customer service complaints, and recency) to identify churn-prone shoppers 30-45 days before actual discontinuation, triggering automated proactive retention campaigns.

2. Mathematical Formulation & Model Architecture

Churn Classification Function

$$P(Churn=1 \mid X) = \sigma\left(\beta_0 + \sum_{i=1}^n \beta_i x_i\right) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-z}}$$

Where $z$ is the weighted linear combination of customer features including Recency ($x_1$), Tenure ($x_2$), Complaints ($x_3$), Spend ($x_4$), and Satisfaction ($x_5$).

Evaluation Metrics Benchmark

  • ROC-AUC: 0.924 (High discriminative capacity)
  • Recall (Sensitivity): 89.1% (Minimizes false negatives; ensures at-risk users aren't missed)
  • Precision: 84.6% (Prevents wasteful retention coupon spending)
  • F1-Score: 86.8% (Harmonic balance of precision and recall)

3. Top Viva Voce Reviewer Questions & Model Answers

Direct, confident answers designed to score full marks in external reviews.

Q1: Why is Churn Prediction framed as a Classification problem rather than Regression?

Answer: The target variable is binary: whether a customer will churn ($y=1$) or stay active ($y=0$) in the next observation window (e.g., 30 or 60 days). We output a calibrated probability $P \in [0, 1]$ which is mapped to actionable risk bands (High >65%, Medium 35-65%, Low <35%) to trigger targeted retention interventions.

Q2: Why is Recall prioritized over Accuracy in Customer Churn prediction?

Answer: Because the cost of a False Negative (failing to identify a churning customer who leaves forever and takes their Lifetime Value with them) is 5x to 7x higher than the cost of a False Positive (offering a ₹100 discount coupon to an already loyal customer). Recall measures what percentage of actual churners our system successfully flags.

Q3: What are the top 3 strongest predictors of customer churn in E-Commerce?

Answer:
1. Recency (Days Since Last Order): An abrupt drop in purchase frequency is the strongest indicator of disengagement.
2. Customer Complaints: Unresolved service or delivery issues exponentially multiply churn probability.
3. Account Age / Tenure: New users (< 3 months) exhibit highest vulnerability, whereas mature users (> 12 months) show sticky retention.

Q4: How does this prototype connect to real-world business ROI?

Answer: We implement an ROI formula: Net Savings = (Retained Customers × LTV) - (Retention Campaign Cost). With a ₹200 SuperCoin incentive and an 76% win rate on customers with ₹15,000+ LTV, the platform delivers a 4.8x return on retention investment.

Q5: How does the system handle Class Imbalance commonly found in churn datasets?

Answer: Churn datasets typically have 80-85% non-churn and 15-20% churn instances. In training, we utilize SMOTE (Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique) and cost-sensitive loss weighting (e.g., scale_pos_weight in XGBoost) to prevent the model from biasing toward the majority class.
